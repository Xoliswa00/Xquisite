# TikTok: Inspiration photos on bookings

**The feature:** when a client books on the business's booking page, the confirm
step has an optional "Have a look in mind?" section. They add up to 3 photos
(Instagram or Pinterest screenshots, camera roll) and a short "Describe the look"
note. They can add or remove photos later from My Bookings until the appointment
starts. The business sees "The look they want" on the appointment page with
thumbnails (tap for full size) and the note. Photos are private to that client and
the business's staff, and location data is stripped. Unsaved photos are deleted 90
days after the appointment. Owners can switch it off per service. Included in
every plan. Staff are notified (in-app plus browser push) if a client adds photos
after booking. After the appointment, staff can save up to 3 "after" photos as the
client's look, and the client can tap "Book this look again".

**Audience:** SA nail techs, lash and brow artists, braiders, hair stylists,
barbers and makeup artists. The felt pain: "send me a pic" messages on personal
WhatsApp, and the client who shows you the look only once they're in the chair.

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920. About 20 to 35 seconds. Hook on screen at
0:00, spoken hook by 0:02. Burned-in captions. Trending audio low under a
voiceover, or straight to camera. Soft CTA.

## Script 1: "She showed me the pic after I started" (to camera + screen inserts)

**Hook (on screen + spoken):** She said "just a small trim." Then she showed me a
picture of a completely different style.

**Body (voiceover):**

> Every nail tech and stylist has had this client. The look only comes out once
> they're already in the chair, and now your whole afternoon has shifted.
>
> Or you ask for a pic on WhatsApp the day before, and she never replies.
>
> On my booking page, clients now get a "Have a look in mind?" box. *[screen: the
> confirm step, the "Have a look in mind?" section]*
>
> They add up to 3 photos and describe the look. Instagram screenshot, Pinterest,
> camera roll. *[screen: tapping Add photos, previews appearing]*
>
> I open the appointment and it's right there, under "The look they want". *[screen:
> the appointment page, thumbnails and the note]*
>
> So I know what time to set aside and which products to pull before she sits
> down.

**CTA:** Link in bio if you're tired of surprises at the chair.

- "Also client: shows me a totally different style"

## Script 2: "POV" (screen recording only, no face, trending audio)

**Text at 0:00:** POV: your client sends the look when she books, not when she
sits down.

**Screen sequence (silent, audio over the top), 390px wide phone view:**
1. The booking page, confirm step. Scroll to **Have a look in mind?**
2. Tap **Add photos**. Pick two demo images (a nail set, a braid style). Previews
   appear.
3. Type in **Describe the look**: *Almond shape, chrome tips, short.* Confirm the
   booking.
4. Cut to the business side: open the appointment. Scroll to **The look they
   want**. Tap a thumbnail to open it full size.
5. Text on screen: *No "send me a pic" on WhatsApp.*
6. Quick cut to **My Bookings**: remove one photo, add another. Text: *She can
   still change it until the appointment starts.*

**End card text:** Up to 3 photos. Private to you and her. Unsaved photos gone after 90 days.

## Posting notes

- **Script 2 is the fastest to make.** Screen recording plus text, no camera.
- **Open Script 1 on the "just a small trim" line.** Try a nail version and a
  braids version and post whichever feels truer to the person filming.
- **Pin a comment** with the app link and the line "Switch it off per service if
  it doesn't suit, like consultations."
- **Use demo data only.** Record from the seeded "Marigold" demo tenant with stock
  or demo inspiration photos. Never show a real client's photos, since these are
  often pictures of the person.
- **Mention the notification** if it fits: staff get an in-app notification and a
  browser push when a client adds photos after booking.
- **Deletion wording:** unsaved photos are deleted after 90 days, saved looks are
  kept 2 years and the client can remove them. Say "photos you don't save" if you
  mention deletion.
- **Series potential:** "stop being surprised in your own salon" with booking
  terms, staff logins and inspiration photos. Script 4 doubles as the retention
  piece for regulars.
