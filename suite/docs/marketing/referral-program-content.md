# Referral programme — content pack (PR #95)

**The feature:** every tenant gets a referral link (`?ref={tenant_id}` on the
questionnaire). A referred business is tagged and sees a "you were referred"
banner (the referrer is never named). Once that business becomes a real tenant,
an admin processes the reward in one click:

- **Referrer:** 1 free month
- **New business:** 50% welcome discount

Both applied through the existing promo-code system (#87). No new discount
mechanism.

**Why this framing:** "refer a salon, get a free month" is sayable out loud. The
original idea (2% for two months ≈ R4) wasn't worth anyone's effort. Both-sides
means the person you refer also gets something, so there's no awkward "I'm
sending you a link that helps me" — you're doing them a favour too.

## FAQ

**When do I get my free month?**
Once the business you referred is set up and live on Xquisite. It's added to your
account automatically.

**Is there a limit?**
No. Every business that joins through your link is another free month.

**Does the business I refer know it came from me?**
Only if you tell them. The form just shows them they were referred, not by whom.

**What counts as "joining"?**
They apply through your link and get set up as a paying-eligible tenant. A signup
that never activates doesn't trigger the reward.
