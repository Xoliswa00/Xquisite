# Release announcement — Xquisite Creations, September 2026

Two tenant-facing features shipped to production 10 Sep 2026: **Booking Policy**
(PR #98) and **Staff accounts** (PR #103). The invoice PDF was also rebuilt, but
that's a quiet change and only rates a one-line mention.

## Impact check

- **Who's affected:** every tenant. Booking Policy also touches their clients —
  one extra tick at booking, and only if the business switches it on. Staff
  accounts affects owners (they can now create team logins) and staff (they can
  now be given one).
- **Anything break:** no. Booking Policy stays off until a business writes a
  policy and turns on "require acceptance". Staff accounts is a pure addition —
  the owner login keeps working exactly as before.
- **Advance notice:** not needed. Both are additions; telling people after the
  fact is fine.
- **Scale:** medium, two features → release notes + a short how-to for each. No
  rollout plan.

---

## Release notes

**New — Booking Policy.** Set your cancellation, no-show, and deposit terms once
in Account & Settings. Clients see them on the booking screen before they
confirm. Turn on "require acceptance" and a client has to tick to agree before
the booking goes through, and the exact time they agreed is saved on the
appointment, so a dispute later has a record.

**New — Staff accounts.** Give your team their own logins instead of sharing one.
Add a staff member, set their password, and pick a role: Manager (runs the
business) or Employee (day-to-day work, no billing or settings). Everyone signs
in as themselves.

**Also in this release:** the invoice PDF for your Xquisite subscription was
rebuilt — each active module itemised, clearer totals.

## How to use it

### Booking Policy

1. Open **Account & Settings** and scroll to **Booking Policy**.
2. Type your terms in the **Terms & Cancellation Policy** box — cancellations,
   no-shows, deposits, late arrivals.
3. Optional: switch on **require acceptance** so a client must tick to agree
   before the booking goes through.
4. Save. It appears on your booking link straight away.

To check a client agreed: open the appointment. The acceptance time is on it.

### Staff accounts

1. Go to **Staff** (under Settings) and click **Add Staff Member**.
2. Enter their name and email, and set a password. You set it here — there's no
   email invite to wait on.
3. Pick a role:
   - **Manager** — everything an Employee can do, plus running the business:
     settings, staff, reports.
   - **Employee** — bookings, customers, and their day-to-day work. Not billing
     or settings.
4. Save. They sign in at the same web address with their own email and password.

Once your team has their own logins, stop sharing the owner login — you'll be
able to see who did what.
