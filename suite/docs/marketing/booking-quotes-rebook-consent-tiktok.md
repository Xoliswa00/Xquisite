# TikTok: Quotes from photos, rebook reminders and sharing consent

**The features** (PR #165, live; see `booking-quotes-rebook-consent-content.md`):

1. **Quotes from photos.** On a service the owner ticks "Confirm price and time from
   the client's photos". The client books and adds photos, the slot is held, and the
   client sees "Price and time are confirmed from your photos". Staff get a "Quote
   needed" alert and send a price, time needed and a note from the booking page. The
   client gets "Your quote is ready" in the app, by push and by email, and taps
   **Accept quote** or **Decline** in My Bookings. Accepting updates the booking's
   time on the calendar and its price at checkout. Declining cancels with nothing to
   pay.
2. **Rebook reminders.** "Remind clients to rebook after N days" on each service.
   One "Time for your next {service}?" message (in-app, push, email) when it's due.
   Leads to "Book this look again" if there's a saved look. Skips clients who
   already rebooked and never messages the whole old client list on switch-on.
   One-click opt-out from the email, or in My Bookings.
3. **Client consent for sharing a look.** Each saved look asks "Happy for {business}
   to share this look?" with Yes, share it / Stop sharing. Staff see "OK to share:
   {client} agreed on {date}" or "Not cleared for sharing", plus a "Consent
   history" on the customer record.

All included in every plan.

**Audience:** SA braiders, nail techs, lash and brow artists, hair stylists,
barbers and salon owners. Felt pains: quoting in DMs at night, long styles booked
into short slots, regulars who drift, and posting client photos with no record of a
yes.

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920. About 25 to 35 seconds. Hook on screen at
0:00, spoken hook by 0:02. Burned-in captions. Trending audio low under a voiceover,
or straight to camera. Soft CTA.

**Lead with Script 1 (quotes).** It's the one that is hardest to find elsewhere.

## Script 3: "Where did she say yes?" (to camera + screen inserts) CONSENT

**Hook (on screen + spoken):** "Can I post it?" "Yes!" Three months ago. On
WhatsApp. Somewhere.

**Body (voiceover):**

> Every salon posts client photos. Almost nobody can find the yes.
>

> {business} to share this look"]*
>

> appointment page, the green line]*
>
> If she hasn't agreed it says "Not cleared for sharing". *[screen: the grey line]*
>
> And she can tap Stop sharing any time. *[screen: Consent history list]*

**CTA:** Link in bio.

**On-screen text beats:**

- "She's asked on the saved look"

## Posting notes

- **Post Script 1B first.** It's screen recording plus text, no camera, and it
  shows the whole flow. Then 1A to camera a few days later.
- **Open 1A on the 10pm DM.** Try a braids version and a nails version and post
  whichever feels truer to the person filming.
- **Don't name other apps.** Keep it about the flow, not comparisons.
- **Pin a comment** on the quotes video: "Tick it on any service. The ones you
  don't tick work exactly as before."
- **Use demo data only.** Record from the seeded "Marigold" demo tenant with stock
  or demo inspiration photos. Never show a real client's photos.
- **The example figure on screen** (for example "R1,450 for about 8 hours") is a
  demo number. Don't present it as a standard price.
- **Consent video wording.** "A record of what she agreed to." Never "POPIA
  compliant".
- **Series potential:** "stop being surprised in your own salon" across booking
  terms, staff logins, inspiration photos and now quotes. The whole arc is
  look in, price out, regular comes back.
