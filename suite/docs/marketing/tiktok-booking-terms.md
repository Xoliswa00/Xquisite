# TikTok — Terms & cancellation policy at booking time (PR #98)

**The feature:** a business writes its cancellation/terms policy once in Account &
Settings and switches on "require acceptance". Every client then sees the policy
on the booking confirm step and must tick a box to agree before they can book.
The system stamps the exact time they accepted onto the appointment, and it shows
on the admin appointment detail page. So no-show and deposit disputes finally have
a record.

**Audience:** SA salon / beauty / wellness / service owners. The felt pain: a
client no-shows, then disputes the deposit with "you never told me about a
cancellation fee," and the owner has no proof.

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920. 21–35 seconds. Hook on screen at 0:00,
spoken hook by 0:02. Burned-in captions. Trending audio low under a voiceover, or
straight to camera. Soft CTA, not a hard sell. Add platform-native emojis to
taste — left out here.

## Script 2 — "POV" (screen recording only, no face, trending audio)

**Text at 0:00:** POV: a client's about to no-show you, but this time you're
covered.

**Screen sequence (silent, audio over the top):**
1. Settings screen. Type a policy into the box: *"Cancellations under 24 hours:
   50% of the service fee. No-shows: charged in full."*
2. Flip the toggle: *"Require clients to accept before booking."*
3. Cut to the client's phone — the booking confirm screen. Policy visible, a
   checkbox: *"I've read and accept the cancellation policy."* The client ticks
   it and books.
4. Cut to the admin appointment detail. A quiet line: *"Terms accepted · 6 Sep
   2026, 14:32."*
5. Text on screen: *when she says "you never told me"* — slow zoom on the
   timestamp.

**End card text:** Proof beats argument.
