# Founding 20 Programme — content pack

Source of truth for this copy: `resources/views/founding-twenty/*.blade.php`,
`app/Http/Controllers/FoundingTwentyController.php`, `config/founding_twenty.php`.
Everything below matches what the code actually does — no invented perks.

**What the programme is:** 20 South African service businesses get three months on
the full platform, free, with no setup fee. In return they use it as their real
booking system and give feedback while the product is still being built. Standard
price afterwards is R200/month, only if they choose to continue. Selection is
curated (a scored questionnaire), not first-come. A R100 fully refundable deposit
holds a spot — asked for **only after** a business is selected.

## The offer in one line

Xquisite Creations is giving 20 small service businesses three months on the full
platform, free, in exchange for using it properly and telling us what works.

### What you get

- **Three months free** — the whole platform: bookings, customers, staff,
  payments, POS, reporting. Not a stripped-down trial tier.
- **No setup fee.** We help you move your existing bookings and customer list
  across.
- **A direct line to the people building it** — on WhatsApp, not a ticket queue.
- **Your data stays yours.** If you leave after three months, you can export
  everything.
- **After three months: R200/month if you want to carry on.** Nothing is charged
  automatically. You decide.

### Who it's for

- You run a salon, beauty, wellness, fitness, or other appointments-based
  business in South Africa.
- Your bookings currently live across WhatsApp, a paper diary, or your memory.
- You have enough regular customers that the admin is starting to cost you real
  time.
- You're ready to actually use a new system for three months, not just sign up
  for one.

### What we ask in return

- Use Xquisite as your real booking system for the three months, not a side
  experiment.
- Tell us what's confusing, what's missing, and what you'd never use. Roughly one
  short conversation a month.
- Let us share your experience, with your permission, once you've had a fair run.

### How we choose

This isn't first-come. We read every questionnaire and pick the 20 businesses we
can help the most and learn the most from.

The questionnaire takes about 5 to 7 minutes. It asks how you run things now:
bookings, customers, payments, and what wastes your time. Every answer shapes what
we build next.

If you're selected, we'll contact you on your preferred method within a few days.

### The R100 deposit, explained

- You pay nothing to apply.
- If you're selected, we ask for a R100 reservation deposit to hold your spot.
- It's **fully refundable** — you get it back. It just filters for people who are
  serious about using the platform for the three months.
- We send banking details and a reference on WhatsApp. You pay, upload the proof,
  and you're in.

### What happens after you apply

1. You submit the questionnaire.
2. We review every application and select 20 businesses.
3. Selected? We reach out, and you pay the R100 refundable deposit to reserve your
   spot.
4. We help you set up and move your existing bookings and customers across.
5. You run your business on Xquisite for three months, free.

### FAQ

**What does it cost after three months?**
R200/month if you choose to continue. Nothing happens automatically — we won't
charge you without you agreeing first.

**Is the R100 deposit safe?**
Yes. It's fully refundable and only asked for once you've been selected. It's a
commitment check, not a fee.

**What if I'm not selected?**
Nothing lost. You can still start a normal free trial, and we keep your answers on
file for the next round.

**Do I need to be good with technology?**
No. If you can use WhatsApp and a banking app, you can use Xquisite. We set it up
with you.

**What about the bookings I already have in WhatsApp or a diary?**
We help you bring your customer list and upcoming bookings across as part of
setup.

**Can I stop?**
Any time. The three months are free with no lock-in. If you stop, you can export
your data.

**Is my business information private?**
Yes. We use your answers to evaluate your application and run the programme, as
set out in our Privacy Policy. We don't sell it.

**Why only 20?**
So we can give each business real attention and act on the feedback properly.
Twenty is what we can support well right now.
